How it works
  • Hit a tyre or another car and it's over. Oil spins you, debris damages the front wing.
  • Tyres wear faster the harder you push. Past ~65% wear grip falls off a cliff; at 100% you puncture.
  • Softs are quick and short-lived, hards are slow and last. Inters and wets only work in the rain.
  • A pit window opens every ~26 s. Steer up into the green gap to box; pick the compound with 1–5 first.
  • Sometimes the wheel gun jams. This is Ferrari.
  • Score = metres travelled + 60 per overtake + 25 per close call.
