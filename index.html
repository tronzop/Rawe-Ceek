How it works
  • Hit a tyre or another car and it's over. Oil spins you, debris damages the front wing.
  • Tyres wear faster the harder you push. Past ~65% wear grip falls off a cliff; at 100% you puncture. Fresh tyres are cold for a few seconds.
  • Softs are quick and short-lived, hards are slow and last. Inters and wets only work in the rain — and at Spa, it rains.
  • A pit window opens every ~26 s. Press B or tap the BOX BOX button (or steer up into the green gap) to box; pick the compound with 1–5 first.
  • In the box you are the pit crew: four wheels, one at a time. Fire the gun (Space, B or tap) when the sweeping marker is in the green — the cyan centre is a perfect. Miss and the nut cross-threads (+1.3 s). Some wheels are jammed with a tiny zone. Under 2.0 s is a record stop (+100); a clean stop is +50.
  • Sit directly behind a rival for the tow: extra speed and the battery charges. Some rivals defend.
  • Safety car: slow down and do not overtake, or it's a 5 s penalty. The restart pays double for overtakes.
  • Every 1.5 km is a Grand Prix: +150 and 25 championship points, then the calendar moves on. Eight venues, two of them at night.
  • Your team-mate is on track too. Passing him is worth extra. The wall will not be happy.
  • Score = metres travelled + 60 per overtake + 25 per close call + bonuses.
