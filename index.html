How it works
  • The start: you are P20 on a staggered grid. Five red lights, then out — ▶ or Space within a third of a second is a +50 great start. Going early is a jump start (no bonus). For ten seconds nothing spawns and a touch in the pack only costs bodywork.
  • Damage: the front wing (debris, nose-to-tail contact) costs top speed; the floor (side-by-side rubs) costs grip. A gentle touch is survivable; a hard frontal hit, or any hit on a part already at 100 %, is a crash. A rolling tyre or a stranded car is always a crash. Oil spins you. The pit crew fixes everything with the tyres.
  • Tyres wear faster the harder you push. Past ~65% wear grip falls off a cliff; at 100% you puncture. Fresh tyres are cold for a few seconds.
  • Softs are quick and short-lived, hards are slow and last. Inters and wets only work in the rain — and at Spa, it rains.
  • A pit window opens every ~26 s. Press B or tap the BOX BOX button (or steer up into the green gap) to box; pick the compound with 1–5 (or the TYRE button) first.
  • In the box you are the pit crew: four wheels, one at a time. Fire the gun (Space, B, ERS or tap) when the sweeping marker is in the green — the cyan centre is a perfect. Miss and the nut cross-threads (+1.3 s). Some wheels are jammed with a tiny zone. Under 2.0 s is a record stop (+100); a clean stop is +50.
  • Sit directly behind a rival for the tow: extra speed and the battery charges. Some rivals defend.
  • Safety car: do not overtake while the field bunches up (5 s penalty), then the race is neutralised and the car drives itself behind the safety car. Your job is the weave: swing the car up and down (▲▼ or wiggle a thumb) to keep the tyre temperature in the green window — too little and you restart on cold tyres with no grip, too much and they blister. A 3-2-1 countdown hands the car back at the green; hit the throttle right after it (not before) for a jump bonus. The restart pays double for overtakes.
  • Every 1.5 km is a Grand Prix: +150 and 25 championship points, then the calendar moves on. Fourteen venues, three of them at night.
  • Your car: pick a team on the title screen. Each car handles differently — top speed, launch, handling, tyre life, battery — plus quirks: some are tough, some are fragile, some have a pit crew that jams the wheel gun. The other car in your colours is your team-mate.
  • Your team-mate is on track too. Passing him is worth extra. The wall will not be happy.
  • Score = metres travelled + 60 per overtake + 25 per close call + bonuses.
